Morphont Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026. Applies to Morphont on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and the web (PWA).

Morphont is a glyph editor. It collects nothing.

What Morphont stores

Everything you make — glyphs, their anchors, guides, ghosts and metrics — is stored only on your device:

  • Android: the app's private storage.
  • Desktop: your user app-data folder (%APPDATA%\Morphont on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Morphont on macOS, ~/.local/share/morphont on Linux).
  • Web: your browser's IndexedDB for this site.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

What Morphont sends

Nothing. Morphont has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry, and the Android app requests no permissions — not even internet access. The web version is served as static files by GitHub Pages; after it loads, it works offline and sends nothing back. GitHub's own handling of page requests is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Files you open or save

When you import a font or project, or export a glyph or project, Morphont reads or writes only the file you pick in your system's file dialog. Fonts you load as reference ghosts stay in memory for that session and are not stored or transmitted.

Children

Morphont collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published here, with a new effective date, and in the repository's history.

Contact
